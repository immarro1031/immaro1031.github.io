Hello I'm Ignacio Marro at Syracuse University.

I am a BDJ student who works at WAER and for the SU Athletic Department.

I'm Ignacio Marro, a sophomore at Syracuse University's S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications, majoring in Broadcast and Digital Journalism. I'm originally from Westfield, New Jersey, and have a passion for storytelling, photography, sports, and current events. At Syracuse, I work with WAER, the university's NPR-affiliated radio station, and as a photographer with the Syracuse Athletics Creative Team. I'm interested in how journalism can combine reporting, data, and visual storytelling to make complicated topics easier to understand. Outside of journalism, I'm involved in Psi Upsilon fraternity, where I'm the house DJ and play drums in our house band. I also enjoy live music, traveling, and photography. I'm always looking for new ways to tell stories and connect with people through my work.

My three favorite things about Syracuse:

  • Dinosaur Bar-B-QUE
  • The Pi Chapter of Psi Upsilon
  • SU's storied basketball legacy
